# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Chromaticity is a small, high-trust team. We commit to creating a
professional, focused, and respectful working environment for every
contributor, reviewer, and user of the project. This Code of Conduct
applies to all project spaces: this repository, issue tracker, pull
requests, code review comments, commit messages, release communications,
and any other forum operated under the Chromaticity name.

## Expected behavior

- Assume competence and good faith. Challenge ideas, not people.
- Prefer written, asynchronous communication that a future reader can
  reconstruct from the record.
- Credit sources, prior art, and co-authors accurately. Clean-room
  discipline (see `docs/29-cleanroom-legal-policy-baseline.md`) is a
  social contract, not only a legal one.
- Accept focused, specific feedback without taking it personally.
- When you disagree, propose an alternative with reasoning, not only an
  objection.
- Disclose conflicts of interest before a review, not after.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, harassment, or intimidation directed at any
  contributor, reviewer, or user.
- Discrimination on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin,
  gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion,
  disability, age, or any other protected characteristic.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or decision processes.
- Publishing others' private information (e.g. physical or email address)
  without explicit permission.
- Any action that undermines the clean-room process, provenance trail,
  or security posture documented in the planning corpus.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.

## Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces and also applies when an
individual represents the project in public spaces (e.g. a talk, a social
media post, a mailing list reply). It applies equally to the Accountable
Owner, the Deputy, contracted specialists (legal, release engineering,
UX), and any external contributor granted access.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior should be sent privately to the
Accountable Owner at **conduct@chromaticity.app**. If the report concerns
the Accountable Owner, send it to the Deputy instead. Reports are
treated as confidential; the Accountable Owner (or Deputy for reports
about the AO) records a private incident record with the minimum
information required to act.

The AO (or Deputy) will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 3 business days.
2. Review the report and gather additional context as needed.
3. Decide on an outcome: no action, private warning, public warning,
   temporary suspension of access, or permanent removal from the
   project.
4. Communicate the decision to the reporter and the subject in writing.

Retaliation against a reporter acting in good faith is itself a Code of
Conduct violation and is grounds for permanent removal.
